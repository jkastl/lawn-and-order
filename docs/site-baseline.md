# Front yard site baseline (v0, Nov 6, 2026)

Working reference for this project. Measurements are scaled from an aerial photo and calibrated to the recorded lot area (±2–3 ft). Street View confirms the layout. Verify with a tape before committing to bed edges.

## Lot
- Rochester, MN. Single family, split-entry.
- About 7,700 sq ft, roughly 68 ft of frontage × 114 ft deep. Rotated ~9° from true north. The house faces the street to the west. The garage is at the north end, and the front door is just south of it, up a short stoop. The lawn slopes gently down to the sidewalk.
- Plan convention: "plan north" up, street on the left. Lot frame in feet: x runs east from the front property line, y runs south from the north line.

The front yard holds about 2,050 sq ft of lawn. Most of the open front lawn is already a clover-rich bee lawn, but the clover is slow to start in spring, so early bloomers fill that gap. Existing beds run along the whole house front, with shrubs and hostas.
- T1 (near the stoop and walk) has been removed. A low stump remains and won't be ground out.
- T2: city boulevard tree just south of the driveway apron (elm or hackberry look).
- T3: the neighbor's boulevard tree by their driveway (honeylocust or Kentucky coffeetree look).
- T4: ~20 ft tree on the lot near the south end of the house.
- T5: tall tree on the neighbor's side of the privacy fence.
- South line: the neighbor's 6 ft wood privacy fence, starting ~10 ft back from the sidewalk.
- A dead colony's beehive sits on the south side of the house. The AC unit is at the back, by the deck (out of scope).

## Rules and programs found (Nov 2026)
- Boulevard: anything other than turf needs a free Natural Landscape Permit from Rochester Forestry. Keep vegetation 18 in. from the sidewalk and street, with 8 ft clearance over sidewalks. No work on boulevard trees without a permit (licensed companies only). Private yard needs no permit (MN Stat. 412.925; Rochester Code 8-5, amended July 2024). The state law doesn't cover HOAs.
- Lawns to Legumes: Spring 2027 cycle, up to $400, apply by **Nov 30, 2026**. Apply in the property owner's name.
- Rochester Garden Grants: up to $1,000, 25% match. Apply **Oct 1–Dec 31** for 2027 installs. The applicant must own the parcel (or be a renter with landlord consent). Not in the ROW. Contact the coordinator listed on the city's Garden Grants page.
- Rochester Rain Barrel cost-share: 90% up to $100, currently only in Cascade Creek pilot neighborhoods (Kutzky, Washington, Lowertown).
- The city gives away topsoil and mulch for a donation. Olmsted County compost site suggests a $20 donation per pickup load.

## Budget v0
- Base plan $1,290–$2,000 over three seasons, about $890–$1,600 net of Lawns to Legumes. Optional extras (soil test, rain barrel kit, stump bee bath, crocus, lawn violets, aerator, boulevard Option B) add $450–$730. Plugs are most of the cost (~575 plugs). About 14 cu yd of free mulch is needed for smothering.
